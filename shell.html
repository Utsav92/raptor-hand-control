How the controls work

Hold an open palm for a moment to separate the assemblies, or a fist to reassemble them. Intermediate finger positions preserve the last stable state.

When tracking begins, your palm position becomes the neutral pose. Turning it shows other sides of the engine; tilting it changes elevation. Moving left, right, up or down moves the model. Moving closer or farther changes its depth.

Choose “Selected assembly,” then choose a part under Inspect, to manipulate that part independently. Changing the selected part recenters the hand automatically.

Camera frames are processed on this computer in a worker. Neither frames nor landmarks are sent to a server. Stop turns off the camera.
